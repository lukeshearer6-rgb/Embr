# Incorporation and domain (task R2)
Question: What does it cost, and what must Luke do, to incorporate Embr as a UK private limited company and buy a domain, using only official sources?
Answer in short: Online incorporation with Companies House costs £100 and is "usually registered within 24 hours". The director must verify identity (free). After that Embr owes a £50 confirmation statement fee, annual accounts and a Company Tax Return, and an ICO data protection fee (the ICO's own pages disagree on the tier 1 price: £52 or £40). Cheapest verified domain is a .co.uk at Porkbun for $5.66 (USD, same renewal). Cloudflare and Namecheap prices for the domains Embr wants could not be verified. Availability of embr.co.uk / embr.com could not be checked.
All figures checked 2026-11-25. Not legal or tax advice. Nothing was bought, signed up for or contacted.

## Findings

### 1. Incorporating
- Online incorporation fee is £100, payable by debit or credit card. "It costs £100 and can be paid by debit or credit card." [Register your company](https://www.gov.uk/limited-company-formation/register-your-company), checked 2026-11-25
- Timing: "Your company is usually registered within 24 hours." [Register your company](https://www.gov.uk/limited-company-formation/register-your-company), checked 2026-11-25
- Fee table: Incorporation £100 online (software £100, paper £124). Same-day incorporation £156 (software column only; no online same-day fee shown). Page "Updated 25 September 2026"; the page gives no effective dates per fee. [Companies House fees](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/companies-house-fees/companies-house-fees), checked 2026-11-25
- Steps (from the 9-step guide): choose company type (limited by shares); appoint at least one director; at least one shareholder (can be the director); identify people with significant control (PSC); prepare memorandum and articles of association; keep company and accounting records; choose and check the company name and trade marks; register with Companies House, then add Corporation Tax services to the business tax account. "You must appoint a director but you do not have to appoint a company secretary." "You need at least one shareholder, who can be a director." [Set up a limited company](https://www.gov.uk/set-up-limited-company), checked 2026-11-25
- Registered office and SIC code: "You'll need to register an official address and choose a SIC code - this identifies what your company does." Detailed rules for registered office and email address sit on a separate page that was not opened: unverified, needs measuring. [Register your company](https://www.gov.uk/limited-company-formation/register-your-company), checked 2026-11-25
- Identity verification: directors and PSCs must verify. "If you become a director, you'll need to provide it as part of your appointment filing or when you incorporate a company" ("it" is the Companies House personal code you get after verifying). The official service is "free of charge". Routes: GOV.UK One Login-based service (mobile app, online security questions, or photo ID at a participating Post Office), or an Authorised Corporate Service Provider (an ACSP may charge). [Verifying your identity for Companies House](https://www.gov.uk/guidance/verifying-your-identity-for-companies-house), checked 2026-11-25
- "You will need to register your company before you start trading." A company can stay dormant. [Limited company formation](https://www.gov.uk/limited-company-formation), checked 2026-11-25

### 2. Ongoing duties and costs
- Confirmation statement: £50 online ("Confirmation statement fee (with your first statement in the 12 month payment period)"; paper £110). [Companies House fees](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/companies-house-fees/companies-house-fees), checked 2026-11-25
- Annual accounts: first accounts due "21 months after the date you registered with Companies House"; later accounts "9 months after your company's financial year ends"; Company Tax Return due "12 months after your accounting period for Corporation Tax ends". The page states no filing fee and no penalty amounts ("There are penalties for filing late with Companies House"). [Prepare and file annual accounts](https://www.gov.uk/prepare-file-annual-accounts-for-limited-company), checked 2026-11-25
- Corporation Tax registration: the step is "Add Corporation Tax services to your business tax account" after registering. The official deadline and any fee for registration were not found (the dedicated gov.uk page failed to load): unverified, needs measuring. [Set up a limited company](https://www.gov.uk/set-up-limited-company), checked 2026-11-25
- ICO data protection fee. "Organisations (including sole traders) that use personal information need to pay a data protection fee" unless exempt. [ICO data protection fee](https://ico.org.uk/for-organisations/data-protection-fee/), checked 2026-11-25
  - ICO fee guide: Tier 1 (turnover up to £632,000 or no more than 10 staff) "The fee for tier 1 is £52"; tier 2 £78; tier 3 £3,763; direct debit "automatic discount of £5". [ICO fee guide](https://ico.org.uk/fee-guide), checked 2026-11-25
  - CONFLICT: another ICO page says "The fee for tier 1 is £40 per year or £35 if you choose to pay by direct debit." [ICO: how much will I need to pay](https://ico.org.uk/for-organisations/data-protection-fee/how-much-will-i-need-to-pay/), checked 2026-11-25. Which is current is unverified. This brief uses £52 (higher) for the total. Luke should confirm on the ICO self-assessment before paying.
  - Whether a pre-trading company with no personal data yet owes the fee, and when the first payment is due: unverified, needs measuring.

The domains list page also gave .com $11.81 and .co.uk $5.66 for both first year and renewal, "All prices are in USD". [Porkbun domains](https://porkbun.com/products/domains), checked 2026-11-25
- Namecheap: .com first year "36% OFF $27.96" (1 year, currency not named on the page); renewal price not shown. [Namecheap .com](https://www.namecheap.com/domains/registration/gtld/com/), checked 2026-11-25. .co.uk price: unverified (page failed to load).

[Cloudflare Registrar](https://www.cloudflare.com/products/registrar/), checked 2026-11-25. Prices: unverified, needs measuring. .co.uk: its docs confirm only transfers ("Cloudflare currently supports the transfer of .uk, co.uk, org.uk, and me.uk domains") and do not say new .co.uk registrations are offered. [Cloudflare UK domains](https://developers.cloudflare.com/registrar/top-level-domains/uk-domains/), checked 2026-11-25

[Cloudflare: register a domain](https://developers.cloudflare.com/registrar/get-started/register-domain/), checked 2026-11-25. Fits Embr's Cloudflare Pages plan.

## What this means for Embr
- Incorporation is £100 and quick, but Luke must verify his identity first (free, GOV.UK One Login route) and needs a registered office address (a real UK address; rules not verified here) and a SIC code.
- Directors and PSCs are named publicly on Companies House. The address rules and privacy options need checking before Luke uses his home address: unverified.
- Embr will hold talent and brand personal data, so plan for the ICO fee as soon as it processes data. Confirm the tier 1 price (£52 vs £40) at the ICO before paying.
- Cloudflare Registrar is a good fit for DNS with Cloudflare Pages, but its .co.uk support for new registrations and its prices are unverified. Porkbun is the only registrar with a verified .co.uk price here.
- CLAUDE.md gate: incorporation and the domain need Luke's approval; nothing is done yet.
