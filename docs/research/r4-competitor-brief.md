# R4: UK competitor brief (platforms where brands book models and creatives)
Question: Which platforms reachable from the UK let brands or photographers book freelance models and creatives, and how does each charge, handle booking and payment, and check age or ID, according to its own pages?
Answer in short: Of five platforms read, only Collabstr shows a full booking-and-payment flow (buyer pays, funds held, released on approval) with stated percentage fees. StarNow, PurplePort and Model Mayhem are listings and casting-call sites where talent can apply to castings; their own pages read did not state payment handling or prices. Spotlight is an actors' directory for agents and casting professionals. Many items are "unverified: needs measuring" because several pages did not load or did not state them.

Scope and method: platform's own pages only, no third-party reviews, nothing signed up to or contacted. All pages checked 2026-11-26. The fetch tool summarises pages, so figures and quotes are as returned by that tool; re-check the live page before quoting externally. Several pages (StarNow membership and member-benefits, PurplePort join, Model Mayhem upgrade, StarNow models page) failed to load, so prices on those sites are unverified. Internal research only; no comparative claim here is cleared for public copy.

## Findings

### 1. Collabstr (collabstr.com)
- What it is: "Collabstr is a marketplace to find and hire influencers on Instagram, TikTok, and YouTube." Self-serve, creators "manage [their] own brand deals". [FAQ](https://collabstr.com/faq), checked 2026-11-26
- Who pays: Creators: "We take a 15% transaction fee when you make a sale"; "There is no up-front cost." [FAQ](https://collabstr.com/faq), checked 2026-11-26. The pricing page says "Creators pay a separate 15% fee on their payout." Brands: Free plan $0 per month; Pro $249/mo billed annually; Premium $333/mo billed annually; Enterprise / Agency from $1,000/mo billed annually. "Free and Pro: 10% hiring fee", "Premium: 5% hiring fee", Enterprise custom. [Pricing](https://collabstr.com/pricing), checked 2026-11-26. The FAQ states no brand-side percentage; the pricing page FAQ also says "Free and Pro plans charge a 10% fee on each order. Premium reduces that to 5%." No contradiction on a second read.
- Booking: brands "buy listed packages, or send a custom offer"; creators accept or decline; "Influencers are given 72 hours to accept new orders before they automatically expire." "We do not charge you until an influencer accepts your order." [FAQ](https://collabstr.com/faq), checked 2026-11-26. Direct hire: creators do not apply. Campaigns: "Post a campaign and let creators come to you by applying to your campaign" (Pro: 1 per month; Premium and Enterprise: unlimited). [Pricing](https://collabstr.com/pricing), checked 2026-11-26
- Payment: "we collect the payment from the buyer and hold it until the order is complete." Brands have "up to 72 hours once the work has been submitted to ask for a revision or open a dispute". Creators are paid via Dots (over five methods incl. PayPal, CashApp, Venmo); brands pay by Stripe. Exact payout timing: not stated on pages read, unverified: needs measuring. [FAQ](https://collabstr.com/faq), checked 2026-11-26
- ID/age: "verifies the identity of each listed influencer"; no age requirement mentioned on pages read. [FAQ](https://collabstr.com/faq), checked 2026-11-26
- UK: FAQ says "Currently we support over 35 countries" (list not seen). A UK listing page exists: "Top 182 UGC Creators in London, United Kingdom". [London page](https://collabstr.com/top-influencers/user-generated-content/in/united-kingdom/london), checked 2026-11-26. Currency shown: not stated, prices appear in USD on the pricing page. Focus is influencers and UGC, not fashion models.

### 2. StarNow (starnow.com)
- What it is: "Where talent and opportunity connect direct." Casting platform; homepage shows "4,304 roles for all ages, looks, and levels of experience." [Homepage](https://www.starnow.com/), checked 2026-11-26
- Who pays: "Create your free profile" and "Place a free casting call". Paid membership prices: unverified: needs measuring (membership and member-benefits pages did not load). Terms: fees "shall be those fees as set out on the Website from time to time"; StarNow may "increase fees or introduce new prices or charges at any time"; 30-day cancel-and-refund right for subscriptions over one month. [Terms of Service](https://www.starnow.com/terms-of-service/), checked 2026-11-26
- Booking: clients post castings; talent can "view and apply to new jobs" in the app; clients can also "hand-pick from our Talent Directory". Talent apply. [Homepage](https://www.starnow.com/), checked 2026-11-26
- Payment: not stated on the pages read. Terms mention a "billing agent" for refund admin fees, which refers to subscriptions, not bookings. Unverified: needs measuring. [Terms](https://www.starnow.com/terms-of-service/), checked 2026-11-26
- ID/age: "StarNow is intended for persons aged 18 or over" and under-18s "may not operate their own registered profile". No ID check described. [Terms](https://www.starnow.com/terms-of-service/), checked 2026-11-26
- Role/UK: terms put responsibility on the advertiser ("it is your responsibility to meet all legal obligations towards those whom your posting is aimed at"). Governing law: "governed by New Zealand law". UK availability not stated; homepage names ITV and BBC as past partners. Same sources, checked 2026-11-26

### 3. PurplePort (purpleport.com)
- What it is: "a free online portfolio-hosting and networking platform for models, photographers, studios, and other creatives." Owner "PurplePort Limited". [About](https://purpleport.com/about/), checked 2026-11-26
- Who pays: "It's free to join PurplePort." A VIP tier is described in PurplePort's own article (not on its homepage or About page), where "the number of images, casting calls, daily messages etc. are in fact unlimited". VIP price: unverified: needs measuring (join page did not load). Models "decide your modelling rates" and "no longer need to split your rate with an agency". [About](https://purpleport.com/about/); [Homepage](https://www.purpleport.com/); [Article](https://purpleport.com/articles/765/why-purpleport-is-a-better-choice-than-modelmayhem/), all checked 2026-11-26
- Booking: photographers "search for models, post casting calls, and message them"; models "Book shoots by creating or applying for castings." Talent can apply and message. [Homepage](https://www.purpleport.com/), checked 2026-11-26
- Payment: not handled in anything read; the About page links an article "How to get paid modelling work on PurplePort" (not opened). Unverified: needs measuring.
- ID/age: not stated on pages read. Accounts are reviewed ("after account review", About page). Unverified.
- UK: not explicitly stated; company name suffix "Limited" and a London event mention only. Unverified: needs measuring.

### 4. Model Mayhem (modelmayhem.com)
- What it is: "Model Mayhem is the #1 portfolio website for professional models and photographers." [FAQs](https://www.modelmayhem.com/faqs), checked 2026-11-26
- Who pays: "normal membership" plus paid VIP and Premium. FAQ: "We offer you a range of prices depending on how long you sign up." Specific prices: unverified: needs measuring. Must be an approved member to buy VIP or Premium. [FAQs](https://www.modelmayhem.com/faqs), checked 2026-11-26
- Booking: site hosts casting calls, travel and availability notices; homepage offers "Find your next gig". Castings "which require the responder to make a payment of any kind are not permitted". Talent respond to castings. [FAQs](https://www.modelmayhem.com/faqs); [Homepage](https://www.modelmayhem.com/), checked 2026-11-26
- Payment: site takes membership payments by credit card and PayPal. Payments between members for shoots: not stated. Unverified.
- ID/age: no age requirement stated in what was read; profiles are reviewed for approval. Unverified.
- UK: not stated. Unverified: needs measuring.

### 5. Spotlight (spotlight.com)
- What it is: "Since 1927, Spotlight has been the gold standard for connecting talented performers with the world's most successful agents." Directory for performers, agents and casting professionals in stage and screen. [Homepage](https://www.spotlight.com/), checked 2026-11-26. Adjacent to Embr (actors, not unsigned fashion models); included as the established UK directory.
- Who pays: separate rates for Performers, Young Performers, Graduates and Casting Professionals; amounts not on the page read. Paid yearly or monthly Direct Debit. Prices: unverified: needs measuring.
- Booking/entry: apply to join; "The team reviews each application". Performers need "professional paid performance experience, accredited vocational training, or a recommendation from a Spotlight registered agent." How castings run on the platform: not explained on the page read.
- Payment between talent and casting professionals: not stated. ID/age: not stated beyond a Young Performers category.
- UK: operated by a London-based company with West End casting studios; parent Talent Systems has "global reach" (homepage summary).

## What this means for Embr
- Facts only: StarNow, PurplePort and Model Mayhem each state that talent respond or apply to castings; Collabstr's direct-hire flow has brands buy from a profile with no application, while its campaign flow has creators apply. Embr's locked position (brands search, Embr ranks, brands make offers, talent cannot apply or message for work; Decision (Luke, 2026-11-20)) differs from the first three on that point.
- Embr's mechanics (deposit held, auto sign-off, Stripe ID check, 18+ only) can be compared with what is stated, but only Collabstr states a held-payment flow, and only StarNow states an 18+ rule. Whether the others have escrow or age checks: unverified: needs measuring.
- Fee structure fact: Collabstr charges creators 15% and brands a 10% or 5% fee on paid plans; Embr's decision is 0% from talent and a 10/7/5/5 brand fee (Decision, Luke, 2026-11-23; tiers not final).
- Not covered: Backstage, Casting Networks, Mandy and fashion-specific UK sites; spot checks of those pages were blocked or not run. Add as a follow-up if needed.
