# Routes to review Embr's booking agreement (R3 / D4)
Question: Which 2 or 3 UK routes could review `docs/dev/booking-agreement.md` at zero or low cost, for Luke to choose from?
Answer in short: The best first route is the Business Law Cafe at Manchester Law School (MMU), a free student clinic supervised by pro bono lawyers, aimed at start-ups. Second is the BPP Enterprise Clinic, free commercial advice for start-ups, but its location and intake route are unverified. Third is the Law Society's Find a Solicitor directory, which is free to search but does not state any price for the solicitors listed. None of the pages say anything about employment-agency law (Conduct of Employment Agencies Regulations 2003), so that question needs a specialist and a paid or quoted route is probably needed for it. All pages checked 2026-11-24.

## Recommendation

2. In parallel, ask the BPP Enterprise Clinic (via LawWorks) whether it takes Embr's case, as LawWorks says it covers "reviewing standard terms and conditions".
3. Use Find a Solicitor later for a fixed-fee quote on the employment-agency question specifically, if the clinics decline it. Ask for the price in writing before instructing.

## Findings

### Option 1: Business Law Cafe, Manchester Law School, Manchester Metropolitan University
- Who: Manchester Law School students, supervised by professional lawyers giving their time pro bono. Listed on LawWorks' site.
- Offers: "free assistance and support" for social enterprises, start-ups, charities, entrepreneurs and small businesses. Listed areas: Charity, Contract, Corporate Structure, Data protection, Commercial. The page describes free written legal information.
- Eligibility: Names start-ups and entrepreneurs. The page does not say whether unincorporated bodies qualify and gives no income limits. Unverified: needs confirming with the clinic.
- Cost: Free.
- Fit: Contract and commercial areas match a marketplace agreement. Whether it will review a full document, as opposed to giving general information, is not stated. Employment-agency law is not mentioned.
- How to start: Appointments only; one clinic per term from 6pm. The only route shown is an email and phone number on the LawWorks page (not repeated here). Public page to start from: https://www.lawworks.org.uk/legal-advice/individuals/business-law-cafe
- Source: [LawWorks, Business Law Cafe](https://www.lawworks.org.uk/legal-advice/individuals/business-law-cafe), checked 2026-11-24

### Option 2: BPP Enterprise Clinic (LawWorks Clinics Network)
- Who: Part of the BPP Law Schools free legal advice centre, listed on the LawWorks Clinics Network.
- Offers: "free commercial legal advice to start-ups and small enterprises", including company registration, "reviewing standard terms and conditions", and conflicts with vendors, customers or commercial property owners.
- Eligibility: No formal criteria on the page. Unincorporated status is not mentioned. Unverified.
- Cost: Free.
- Location: Unverified. The page opened does not say which BPP site serves clients, so it may not be Manchester.
- How to start: The page gives no client application route; the address shown is for volunteers and clinic setup only, so do not use it for client requests. Start from the page: https://www.lawworks.org.uk/node/1417 and look for BPP's own intake route. Unverified: needs measuring.
- Source: [LawWorks Clinics Network, Enterprise clinics](https://www.lawworks.org.uk/node/1417), checked 2026-11-24

### Option 3: Law Society "Find a Solicitor" (paid route, for the employment-agency question)
- Who: Free directory of SRA-regulated solicitors, owned by the Law Society of England and Wales.
- Offers: Advanced search can filter by primary and secondary areas of practice. The page does not describe a location filter, free first meetings or fixed fees.
- Eligibility: Anyone; no eligibility test for the directory itself. Each firm sets its own terms.
- Cost: The directory is free ("a free service"). Cost of any solicitor found: unverified: needs measuring (ask each firm for a written quote).
- How to start: https://solicitors.lawsociety.org.uk/ (search by area of practice and Manchester, then check each firm's own site; this step is not yet done).
- Source: [Find a Solicitor, About](https://solicitors.lawsociety.org.uk/about), checked 2026-11-24

### Considered, not shortlisted
- University of Manchester Legal Advice Centre: free, staffed by supervised law students, but its pages list small claims, employment, housing, wills, family, dementia and charity advice, and do not mention businesses or commercial contracts. Could ask, low odds. Request form: https://socialsciences.manchester.ac.uk/legal-advice-centre/services/request-an-appointment/. Sources: [services page](https://socialsciences.manchester.ac.uk/legal-advice-centre/services), [public legal advice page](https://www.manchester.ac.uk/collaborate/communities/legal-advice), checked 2026-11-24.
- LawWorks Not-for-Profits Programme: for not-for-profit organisations with public or community benefit; Embr is a commercial marketplace, so likely ineligible. [Page](https://www.lawworks.org.uk/legal-advice-not-profits/free-legal-assistance), checked 2026-11-24.
- Salford and startup fixed-fee firms: not researched to a page I opened, so not included.
