## 2. 20-minute discovery interview script (brand founders)

For Luke. Open questions only. Do not pitch, do not describe prices, fees or results. If asked about Embr, say you are early and mostly here to learn.

**Warm-up (3 min)**
1. Tell me about the brand and how it started.
2. What are you working on this season?
3. Where do your pictures and videos get used (site, social, shop, press)?

**How they book people today (5 min)**
4. Think about your last shoot. Talk me through it from first idea to final images.
5. Who was in front of the camera and how did you find them?
6. Who else did you need (photographer, stylist, hair, make-up) and how did you find them?
7. What did you use to keep track of it all?
8. How do you decide who is right for a shoot?

**Pain points (4 min)**
9. What part of arranging a shoot takes the most time?
10. What has gone wrong on a shoot, or before one?
11. What do you wish you knew about someone before booking them?

**Budget and payment process (4 min)** (ask, never assume)
12. How do you decide what a shoot can cost?
13. Who signs it off?
14. Once a shoot is done, how do people get paid on your side? What happens, and when?
15. How does that fit with how you pay your other suppliers?
16. What happens if a shoot is cancelled or moved?

**ID/age and usage rights (3 min)**
17. How do you currently check someone's age or identity before a shoot, if at all?
18. How do you agree where and for how long the images can be used?
19. What has been unclear or caused trouble about usage in the past?

**Close (1 min)**
20. If you could change one thing about how you book talent, what would it be?
21. Is there anyone else I should talk to?
22. Thank them. Say what happens next (only that you will write up notes). Do not promise access, pricing or a launch date.
