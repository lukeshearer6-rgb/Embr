# Embr: lessons from real talent contracts (v6.2, 17 Nov 2026)

Luke shared three of his own signed agency-booked contracts (a beer brand campaign via a London creative agency, a German betting brand via a Hamburg production company, and a global soft-drink brand via a large production network). They are big-brand contracts for a signed model, not Embr's core market, so only what fits unsigned talent and smaller brands was taken. Personal details in those contracts were not copied anywhere.

## What the industry does, and what Embr took from it
| Industry practice seen in the contracts | Built into Embr (v6.2) |
|---|---|
| Usage period runs from **date of first use**, not the shoot date; usage fee payable only once used | Usage periods start on first use. Brand confirms the go-live date (board to-do). If nothing goes live within 6 months of the shoot, the period starts then anyway, so brands can't sit on images forever. |
| Talent dropped from the edit ("outgraded") gets no usage fee | Already matched by Embr's final-cut model. Embr is fairer: undecided lines are charged at the deadline. |
| **Pre-priced renewal options** (e.g. +10% per renewal; fixed 6/12-month option fees) | Each usage line can carry a renewal price (defaults to fee +10%). Brand renews in one tap; no talent approval needed because the price was agreed; next renewal price rises 10%. |
| Standard day (10 h incl. 1 h lunch), **overtime** per hour or part (about 20% of the day rate), fitting fees | Offer has "The day": length (8/10/12 h), overtime £/hour (default 20% of day rate), fitting fee. Talent claim overtime when marking wrapped, pre-filled from shoot-day check-in/out times; brand sees it at sign-off; Embr fee applies; talent paid with the balance. |
| **Category exclusivity** naming the category/competitors, usually for the usage period; talent warrants no clash | Exclusivity names the category and can run "for the usage period". **Clash check:** brands see a warning if the talent already has exclusivity with another brand in the same industry (competitor not named); talent see which brand it clashes with before accepting. |
| **Generative AI** clauses: one contract refused any digital cloning; another allowed AI for localisation only, no training | Per-booking AI setting: "No AI changes" (default) or "Edits and localisation only, no training, no new content". Talent set a default on their profile; brands must confirm if they choose more than the talent's preference. |
| Versions/cutdowns/stills from footage included; paid online geolocked, organic global; old posts may stay after expiry but nothing new; archive/awards use forever | Agreement clause 5b spells this out in plain English. |
| Don't change appearance before the shoot; confidentiality; no posting until launch | Clause 10a plus per-booking "When talent can post" (after launch / any time / only with OK). |
| (Missing in the big contracts) talent can't use the images | Embr default: talent may use final images in their portfolio after launch. Important for unsigned talent building a book. |
| Talent is an independent contractor, responsible for own tax | Clause 6b. |

## Deliberately left out (not Embr's market)
Role grading (main vs supporting decided in the edit) · standby/weather days · per diems · passport rules · foreign tax withholding · union framework references (IPA/Equity, Equity TV Commercials Agreement) · holiday-pay split · morals clauses that let a brand withhold pay unilaterally (Embr's dispute process handles that instead) · full buy-outs in perpetuity.

## Agreement clauses added or changed
5b What the usage covers · 5c Renewals · 5d AI · 6b Fees for the day · 7 Exclusivity (category, end date, no-clash warranty) · 10a Appearance, confidentiality and posting · 10b Content · 10c Sign-off. All still need a UK solicitor's review.

## Still open
